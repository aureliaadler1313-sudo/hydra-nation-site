Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Hydra Nation account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Hydra Nation
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to the Danger zone and select Delete account
  4. Confirm — this happens immediately

Option 2: Without the app installed

No longer have Hydra Nation on your device? Email us from the address associated with your account and we'll process the deletion for you.

Request deletion by email

We'll confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days of receiving your request.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (email, authentication record)
  • Your hydration logs, streaks, and history
  • Your alien collection, coins, and game progress
  • Your notification and app preferences

What this doesn't affect

Deleting your account is separate from uninstalling the app. Uninstalling alone does not delete your account or data — use one of the options above to do that.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. For more detail on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
