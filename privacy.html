Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Introduction

Hydra Nation ("App", "we", "us", or "our") operates as a mobile application.

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information Collection and Use

Data We Collect

Authentication Data

  • Email address (for account creation and sign-in via Supabase authentication)
  • Authentication tokens stored securely

Health & Activity Data

  • Daily water intake logs (glasses of water consumed)
  • Hydration streaks and history
  • Timestamps of water log entries

Game Progress Data

  • Alien collection status and progression
  • Coin/currency balance
  • Game level and achievements
  • Preferences for notification settings

Device Data

  • Device identifiers (for authentication and data synchronization)
  • App crash and error logs (for debugging)

Referral and Friend Data

  • Your referral code and the account it belongs to
  • A record of who referred whom, so referral bonuses are awarded once per account
  • Friend connections you create, either by redeeming a referral code or by adding a friend's code manually

Shared Progress Data (only if you turn sharing on)

  • A display name, taken from your account name or the first part of your email address — never the full address
  • The number of glasses you have logged today and your daily goal
  • The calendar date those figures relate to

How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to:

  • Maintain your user account and authentication
  • Store your hydration logs and game progress on your own device
  • Award referral bonuses and show the friends leaderboard, if you have chosen to share
  • Display your progress and statistics
  • Send push notifications for hydration reminders (with your permission)
  • Improve app stability and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored on Supabase, a secure cloud database platform
  • Your authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using secure device storage
  • We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Your account is protected by authentication credentials that only you control

Sharing With Other Users

Hydra Nation includes a friends leaderboard. Sharing turns on when you add your first friend, because a leaderboard where only one of you can be seen does not work. Until you add someone, nothing about your hydration leaves your device. You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings → “Share progress with friends”, and once you have turned it off, adding another friend later will not turn it back on.

While sharing is on, people you are connected to can see:

  • Your display name
  • The glasses you have logged today, and your daily goal

They cannot see your email address, your history from previous days, your game progress, your streaks, or anything else about your account.

What we keep, and for how long. We hold at most two days of your counts — today and yesterday, so the leaderboard can settle who won the day — and anything older is deleted. Separately we keep one small record showing that you share, holding your display name and your daily goal. That record is what lets friends still see you, at zero, before your first glass of the day; it carries no hydration history and lasts until you turn sharing off or delete your account.

If you turn sharing off, both of these are deleted from our servers immediately. They are removed, not hidden, and you stop appearing on other people’s leaderboards straight away.

Friend connections are created when you redeem someone’s referral code, or when you add a friend using their code. Being connected to someone does not by itself reveal any hydration data — that only happens if you have also turned sharing on. Friend connections are removed when either account is deleted.

Local Storage

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data by:

  1. Opening the App
  2. Going to Settings
  3. Selecting "Delete Account"

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Your account is deleted from our servers, along with your referral records, friend connections, and any shared progress. Your hydration logs and game progress are stored on your own device and are erased from it at the same time.

If you no longer have Hydra Nation installed, you can request deletion the same way by emailing infohydranation@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days. See our dedicated account deletion page for details.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete such information promptly.
